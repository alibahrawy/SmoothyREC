# Changelog

## 1.1.1 — 2026-10-23

- Improved repeated Stop, Save & Quit, capture failure recovery and preservation of existing recording files.
- Fixed microphone compression, MP4 container handling and picture-in-picture timing with optional tracks of different lengths.
- Prevented screen previews from restarting after closing the recorder window.
- Added anonymous installation and feature counts with a Settings → General opt-out and `SMOOTHY_TELEMETRY=0` support. Reports exclude recordings, filenames, paths, device details and accounts.
- Official Apple silicon builds are Developer ID signed, notarized and stapled.
